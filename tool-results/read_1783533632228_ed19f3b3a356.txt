  1123→  1123→  1123→// (e.g., "Dengue outbreak in Dhaka — severity high — antimalarials + IV fluids

  1126→  1126→  1126→// and occasion adjustments, because during an outbreak, demand for affected
  1127→  1127→  1127→// products spikes regardless of season.
  1128→  1128→  1128→//
  1129→  1129→  1129→// Pre-seeded with 2 example alerts (inactive by default):

  1131→  1131→  1131→//   - COVID-19 (historical, inactive)
