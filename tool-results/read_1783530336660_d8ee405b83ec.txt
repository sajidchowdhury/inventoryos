  1063→// ── 3. ReportSeason ──
  1064→// Recurring seasonal periods that shift baseline demand for months at a time.
  1065→// Unlike occasions (point events), seasons are extended periods (2-3 months)
  1066→// with a "multiplier" that adjusts the baseline demand for affected product
  1067→// categories throughout the season.
  1068→//
  1069→// Pre-seeded with 4 Bangladesh seasons:
  1070→//   - Winter (Dec-Feb): respiratory meds spike 1.5x
  1071→//   - Summer (Mar-May): ORS, antacids, heatstroke meds 1.3x (overlaps Ramadan/Eid)
  1072→//   - Monsoon (Jun-Sep): dengue/malaria/water-borne disease meds 2.0x
  1073→//   - Autumn (Oct-Nov): transition, baseline 1.0x

  1075→// A season's effect is COMPOUND with occasions: Eid in Monsoon = Eid spike (2.8x)
